Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 August 2026. This page explains plainly what this website collects and why.

Information you choose to provide

When you use a contact or guestbook form, we receive the information you enter, such as your name, email address, optional message, and any optional field on that form. We use it only to respond to you and operate this project.

Forms and service providers

Forms are processed by Formspree. We do not sell personal information. We do not use form details for advertising or build audiences from them.

Analytics and cookies

We may use basic analytics and cookies only after you accept them through the cookie banner where it is shown. You can decline non-essential cookies.

Retention and deletion

We keep submitted information only as long as reasonably necessary to respond and operate the project. To ask for access, correction, or deletion, email interfaith-sanctuary@protonmail.com.

Children

This website is not intended for children under 18. Do not submit personal information for someone else without their permission.

Changes

We may update this policy when the site changes. The latest version will always appear on this page.
